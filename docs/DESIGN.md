## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an airy, modern epicurean sensibility tailored for high-end culinary platforms, boutique dining spaces, and artisanal purveyors. The visual direction balances the crisp editorial restraint of Scandinavian minimalism with the tactile warmth of a sunlit French bistro. 

Key attributes:
- **Atmosphere:** Clean, sunlit, serene, and naturally appetizing. The interface steps back to let vivid food photography, organic textures, and crafted menus take center stage.
- **Target Audience:** Discerning diners, culinary enthusiasts, and luxury lifestyle patrons who expect seamless modern utility paired with boutique charm.
- **Tone:** Inviting without being casual; refined without being sterile. Micro-interactions should feel gentle, paced, and deliberate, mimicking the effortless grace of fine hospitality.

## Colors

The palette revolves around sun-washed organic surfaces, rooted in culinary heritage and botanical freshness.

- **Backgrounds:**
  - Base canvas: `#FDFDFB` (Parchment White) — an ultra-clean, warm ivory base that softens digital glare.
  - Layered surface: `#F6F4EF` (Cultured Cream) — used for nested panels, cards, and quiet differentiation without relying on hard lines.
  - High-elevation surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Light) — applied strictly to floating interactive containers and modal dialogs to capture simulated light.
- **Accents:**
  - **Primary (`#C62828`):** Ripe Cherry. Used sparingly for primary conversion points, active states, cart counts, and price highlights.
  - **Secondary (`#4E7D63`):** Garden Sage. Represents botanical provenance, sustainability notes, dietary certifications, and affirmative states.
  - **Tertiary (`#D99B52`):** Spiced Ochre. Accent tone for seasonal flags, awards, cellar selections, and tasting note highlights.
- **Neutrals:**
  - **Primary Body Text (`#2B2824`):** Roasted Espresso. A deep warm umber that offers stark legibility without the jarring synthetic sharpness of pure `#000000`.
  - **Muted Text (`#68625A`):** Peppercorn Grey. Used for secondary labels, metadata, and dish origins.
  - **Subtle Borders (`rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.08)`):** Soft linen-like dividers preserving visual breathability.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. Its balanced geometric construction and subtle organic curves provide high functional legibility while retaining a friendly, warm personality.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set with tight negative letter tracking to create an intentional, crafted editorial hierarchy on section headers, curated menus, and hero spreads.
- **Body Text:** Ample line heights preserve lightness and readability against the cream canvas, accommodating long-form culinary descriptions and ingredient lists.
- **Uppercase Labels:** The `label-caps` token brings an intentional luxury touch when applied to provenance tags (e.g., "ORGANIC", "ESTATE BOTTLED", "AOC"), table numbers, and micro-headers.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid 12-column grid system is deployed for wide surfaces, prioritizing generous white space to evoke the calm luxury of an uncrowded dining room.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers cap at `1280px` to maintain comfortable eye movement.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Multi-column cards shift to 2-up arrangements.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Horizontal scrolling overflow trays are favored for categories, pairings, and photography carousels.

Spacing should always err on the side of breathing room. Elements within cards and list items lean on deliberate padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to prevent visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse drop shadows tinted with roasted umber tones rather than cold neutral blacks, evoking the natural warmth of gentle overhead lighting.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Surfaces rest on `#FDFDFB` or `#F6F4EF` with no shadow. Structural differentiation occurs via tonal shifts and faint boundaries: `border: 1px solid rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.06)`.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Toggles):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.02)`. Creates a light lift from the cream ground.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -6px rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.07), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.03)`. Surfaces rise subtly with an accompanying 2px vertical transform.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Fixed Bottom Drawers):** `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a diffused backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(253, 253, 251, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces a refined hybrid shape philosophy:

- **Containers & Surfaces:** Employ standard roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` for small containers; `1rem` / `16px` for cards, modals, and product showcases). This establishes a structured, architectural foundation.
- **Interactive Badges & Pills:** Buttons, filter tags, ingredient highlights, and metadata chips deviate to a full pill curve (`border-radius: 9999px`), imparting a soft, approachable, tactile quality.
- **Image Frames:** Culinary imagery uses generous `rounded-lg` corners (`1rem` / `16px`) to soften compositions and echo ceramic dinnerware.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Ripe Cherry (`#C62828`) with white text (`#FFFFFF`). Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal padding. Hover state shifts to `#B71C1C` with an ambient glow shadow (`0 6px 18px -4px rgba(198, 40, 40, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.2)`, text `#2B2824`. On hover, fills with `#F6F4EF` and transitions border to `#2B2824`.
- **Tertiary / Botanical:** Filled in Sage Green (`#4E7D63`) with white text. Reserved for dietary preferences, farm partnerships, and sustainability actions.

### Chips & Tags (Pills)
- Always fully pill-shaped. Height: `28px` to `32px`.
- **Filter Chip (Inactive):** `#F6F4EF` background, `#68625A` text, border `1px solid rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.06)`.
- **Filter Chip (Active):** `#2B2824` background, `#FDFDFB` text.
- **Badge / Dietary Tag:** Soft green tint (`rgba(78, 125, 99, 0.1)`) with `#2E7D32` bold text, or warm cherry tint (`rgba(198, 40, 40, 0.08)`) with `#C62828` text.

### Cards (Menu Items & Artisanal Goods)
- Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- Border: `1px solid rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.06)`.
- Padding: `space-lg` (24px).
- Internal layout: Aspect ratio `4:3` or `1:1` for food photography, followed by `space-md` separation to title, ingredient descriptions in `body-sm` (`#68625A`), and bottom row anchoring price and add-to-order controls.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#F6F4EF`.
- Height: `48px`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.12)`.
- Border-radius: `0.75rem` (12px).
- Focus State: Border color moves to `#C62828` with a soft outer ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(198, 40, 40, 0.12)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Width/Height: `20px`.
- Active state: `#C62828` fill with crisp white checkmark or center pip.
- Inactive state: Border `1.5px solid rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.25)`, background `#FFFFFF`.

### Course / Tasting Menu Lists
- Borderless rows separated by `1px solid rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.07)`.
- Padding: `space-md` top and bottom.
- Layout: Left side features the course number in `label-caps` (`#4E7D63`) above the dish title; right side anchors the price in `headline-sm` with a light dotted leader for classic bistro menus.
